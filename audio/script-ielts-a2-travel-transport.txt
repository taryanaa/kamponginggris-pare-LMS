IELTS A2 Listening Test - Travel and Transportation

[Audio Script - Duration: approximately 3 minutes]

Narrator: You will hear a conversation between Maria and David about their travel plans and different ways to get around the city. Listen carefully and answer questions 16-20.

Maria: Hi David! I'm so excited about our trip to London next week. Have you thought about how we'll get from the airport to the city center?

David: Hi Maria! Yes, I've been researching that. We have several options. We can take the train, the bus, or a taxi from Heathrow Airport.

Maria: What's the cheapest option?

David: The bus is definitely the cheapest. It costs only 6 pounds per person, but it takes about 1 hour and 30 minutes because of traffic.

Maria: That's quite slow. What about the train?

David: The train is much faster - only 45 minutes to reach central London. But it's more expensive, about 25 pounds per person.

Maria: And the taxi?

David: A taxi would be the most convenient, especially with our luggage. It takes about 1 hour depending on traffic, and costs around 60 pounds total for both of us.

Maria: I think the train sounds like the best option - not too expensive and quite fast. Once we're in London, how should we get around the city?

David: London has an excellent public transportation system. The Underground, which people call "the Tube," is the fastest way to travel around the city.

Maria: How much does it cost to use the Tube?

David: If we buy a day pass, it costs 15 pounds per person and we can use it for unlimited trips on the Tube, buses, and trams for the whole day.

Maria: That sounds reasonable. What about walking? I'd like to see the city on foot sometimes.

David: Walking is great for short distances and it's free! Many of London's famous attractions are close to each other. For example, you can walk from Big Ben to the London Eye in just 10 minutes.

Maria: Perfect! What if we want to go somewhere far from the city center?

David: We can use the regular buses. They're slower than the Tube but you can see more of the city. A single bus ticket costs 2.50 pounds.

Maria: I've heard that London has those famous red double-decker buses. Are they still running?

David: Yes! The red double-decker buses are still very popular. Tourists love them. You can even take special sightseeing bus tours that cost about 30 pounds per person.

Maria: That might be fun for one day. What about bicycles? Can we rent bikes in London?

David: Absolutely! London has a bike-sharing system called "Boris Bikes" or "Santander Cycles." You can rent a bike for 2 pounds per day, plus 2 pounds for each 30-minute journey.

Maria: Is it safe to cycle in London?

David: There are many bike lanes now, so it's much safer than before. Just remember to wear a helmet and follow the traffic rules.

Maria: Great! What about going to other cities from London? I'd love to visit Oxford for a day.

David: That's easy! You can take a train from London to Oxford. The journey takes about 1 hour and costs around 20 pounds for a return ticket.

Maria: And if we want to go to Paris?

David: You can take the Eurostar train through the Channel Tunnel. It takes about 2 hours and 30 minutes from London to Paris. Tickets start from 50 pounds if you book in advance.

Maria: That's amazing! We could have a day trip to Paris. What about flying to other European cities?

David: London has several airports. Flights to European cities are quite cheap - you can find tickets for as low as 30 pounds to cities like Amsterdam or Barcelona.

Maria: This is so helpful! I think we'll have many transportation options. Should we book anything in advance?

David: I recommend booking the train from the airport and any long-distance trains in advance. You can get better prices and guaranteed seats.

Maria: Perfect! I can't wait for our London adventure!

David: Me too! It's going to be an amazing trip.

[End of recording]

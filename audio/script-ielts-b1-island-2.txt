IELTS B1 Listening Test - Island Life: Flora and Fauna

Good morning, I'm Dr. James Thompson, a marine biologist, and today I'll be discussing island ecosystems and biodiversity.

Island ecosystems are remarkable for their high levels of endemism, which refers to species that exist nowhere else on Earth. This phenomenon occurs because islands are isolated from mainland populations, allowing unique evolutionary pathways to develop.

The size of an island directly affects its carrying capacity. Larger islands can support more diverse communities of plants and animals, while smaller islands typically have simpler ecosystems with fewer species.

Many island species have evolved without natural predators, making them particularly vulnerable to introduced species that arrive through human activities or natural dispersal.

This unique biodiversity makes island conservation efforts critically important for maintaining global ecological diversity. Thank you for your attention.

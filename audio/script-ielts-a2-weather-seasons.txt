IELTS A2 Listening Test - Weather and Seasons

[Audio Script - Duration: approximately 3 minutes]

Narrator: You will hear a conversation between Anna and Ben about weather patterns and seasonal activities in different countries. Listen carefully and answer questions 21-25.

Anna: Hi Ben! I'm planning to travel around the world next year, and I want to know about the weather in different places. Can you help me?

Ben: Of course, Anna! I love talking about weather and climate. Which countries are you thinking of visiting?

Anna: I want to go to Australia first. What's the weather like there?

Ben: Well, Australia is in the Southern Hemisphere, so their seasons are opposite to ours. When it's winter here in Europe, it's summer in Australia. December, January, and February are the hottest months there.

Anna: That's interesting! What's the temperature like in summer?

Ben: In summer, temperatures in cities like Sydney can reach 35 degrees Celsius or even higher. It's quite hot and humid. But it's perfect for going to the beach!

Anna: And what about winter in Australia?

Ben: Australian winters are quite mild. In June, July, and August, temperatures are usually around 15 to 20 degrees Celsius. It rarely snows, except in the mountains.

Anna: That sounds nice! What about Canada? I heard it gets very cold there.

Ben: Yes, Canada has very cold winters! In cities like Toronto and Montreal, winter temperatures can drop to minus 20 degrees Celsius or even colder. There's lots of snow from December to March.

Anna: Wow, that's freezing! What do people do in such cold weather?

Ben: Canadians love winter sports! They go skiing, ice skating, and ice hockey. Many people also enjoy snowshoeing and sledding. Winter festivals are very popular too.

Anna: What about summer in Canada?

Ben: Canadian summers are actually quite warm and pleasant. Temperatures can reach 25 to 30 degrees Celsius. July is usually the hottest month. It's perfect for camping, hiking, and swimming in lakes.

Anna: I'd also like to visit India. What's the weather like there?

Ben: India has a monsoon climate. There are three main seasons: hot season, monsoon season, and cool season. The hot season is from March to June, with temperatures often above 40 degrees Celsius.

Anna: That sounds extremely hot! When is the monsoon season?

Ben: The monsoon season is from June to September. This is when India gets most of its rain for the whole year. It can rain heavily every day for weeks.

Anna: Is it dangerous?

Ben: The monsoon rains are very important for farming, but sometimes there can be flooding. People usually stay indoors more during heavy rain days.

Anna: And the cool season?

Ben: The cool season is from October to February. This is the best time to visit India because temperatures are comfortable - around 20 to 25 degrees Celsius, and there's very little rain.

Anna: What about the United Kingdom? I know it rains a lot there.

Ben: Yes, the UK is famous for its rainy weather! It can rain at any time of the year, but autumn and winter have the most rain. October and November are usually the wettest months.

Anna: What are the temperatures like?

Ben: The UK has a mild climate. Summer temperatures are usually between 15 and 25 degrees Celsius. Winter temperatures rarely go below zero - usually around 2 to 7 degrees Celsius.

Anna: That doesn't sound too bad. What about spring in the UK?

Ben: Spring is beautiful in the UK! March, April, and May have mild temperatures and lots of flowers blooming. It's a great time to visit gardens and parks.

Anna: Finally, what about Thailand? I heard it's always hot there.

Ben: Thailand has a tropical climate, so yes, it's warm all year round. Temperatures are usually between 25 and 35 degrees Celsius. There are three seasons: hot, rainy, and cool.

Anna: When is the rainy season?

Ben: The rainy season is from May to October. It doesn't rain all day, but there are heavy showers, usually in the afternoon or evening.

Anna: And the cool season?

Ben: The cool season is from November to February. It's still warm, but less humid and more comfortable for tourists. This is the best time to visit Thailand.

Anna: This is so helpful! I think I'll plan my trip according to the best weather in each country.

Ben: That's a smart idea! Good weather can really make your trip more enjoyable.

[End of recording]

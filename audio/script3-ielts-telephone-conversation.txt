**IELTS Level Professional Telephone Conversation Script**

[Phone ringing, then connection sounds]

**Receptionist (professional, polite tone):**
Good morning, International Business Solutions. This is Emma speaking. How may I direct your call?

**Caller (American accent, business professional):**
Hello, Emma. This is Robert Chen from Global Tech Industries. I'm calling regarding the software licensing agreement we discussed last week. Could you please connect me with Ms. Patricia Williams in the Legal Department?

**Receptionist:**
Certainly, Mr. Chen. Let me check if Ms. Williams is available. One moment, please.

[Brief hold music - classical piece]

**Patricia Williams (British accent, formal but warm):**
Hello, Mr. Chen. This is Patricia Williams. Thank you for your patience. I understand you're calling about the software licensing agreement. How can I assist you today?

**Robert Chen:**
Thank you, Ms. Williams. I appreciate you taking my call. I've reviewed the draft agreement you sent over, and I have a few questions regarding the intellectual property clauses. Specifically, I'm concerned about the scope of the usage rights for our international subsidiaries.

**Patricia Williams:**
I see. The current agreement grants usage rights to your parent company and its wholly-owned subsidiaries, provided they operate within the territories specified in Schedule A. Are you looking to extend this to include joint venture partners as well?

**Robert Chen:**
Actually, yes. We have several joint ventures in the Asia-Pacific region that would benefit from access to this software. However, I want to ensure that our proprietary modifications remain protected. Would it be possible to include a clause that explicitly protects any customizations we develop?

**Patricia Williams:**
That's a reasonable request. We can certainly incorporate a provision that maintains your intellectual property rights over any modifications or custom developments. This is quite common in our licensing agreements with multinational corporations.

**Robert Chen:**
Excellent. There's also the matter of the payment schedule. The current terms require payment in full upon execution. Given the substantial investment involved, would you consider a milestone-based payment structure?

**Patricia Williams:**
I understand your position. Let me consult with our finance team regarding this. Typically, for agreements above a certain threshold, we do offer flexible payment options. However, this would require approval from our senior management. May I suggest we schedule a conference call for tomorrow afternoon to discuss the financial terms in detail?

**Robert Chen:**
That would be perfect. I'm available between 2:00 and 4:00 PM Eastern Time. Also, before we conclude, could you clarify the dispute resolution mechanism? I noticed the agreement specifies arbitration in London.

**Patricia Williams:**
Yes, that's correct. We prefer arbitration through the International Chamber of Commerce, with London as the designated seat. This provides a neutral forum and is consistent with our standard international agreements. However, if this presents difficulties for your organization, we could consider alternative venues.

**Robert Chen:**
London should be acceptable, given our European operations. Thank you for clarifying that. I think we've covered the main points. Shall we proceed with the revised draft incorporating our discussion points?

**Patricia Williams:**
Absolutely. I'll have our legal team prepare an amended version by close of business today. You'll receive it via email with track changes enabled so you can easily identify the modifications. Please don't hesitate to contact me if you have any additional questions.

**Robert Chen:**
Thank you very much for your time and expertise, Ms. Williams. I look forward to receiving the revised agreement.

**Patricia Williams:**
You're most welcome, Mr. Chen. Have a productive day.

[Phone call ends with professional disconnect tone]

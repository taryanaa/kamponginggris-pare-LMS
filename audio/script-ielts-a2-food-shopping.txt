IELTS A2 Listening Test - Food and Shopping

[Audio Script - Duration: approximately 3 minutes]

Narrator: You will hear a conversation between Lisa and Tom at a supermarket. They are discussing their shopping list and food preferences. Listen carefully and answer questions 11-15.

Lisa: Hi Tom! Thanks for coming shopping with me today. I have a long list of things to buy.

Tom: No problem, Lisa! I enjoy shopping for food. What do we need to get first?

Lisa: Well, let's start with fruits and vegetables. I need to buy some apples, bananas, and oranges. Do you like any particular fruits?

Tom: I love strawberries! They're my favorite fruit. But they're quite expensive this season. I think they cost about 5 dollars per kilogram.

Lisa: That is expensive! Let's get some cheaper fruits today. How about these bananas? They're only 2 dollars per kilogram.

Tom: Good idea! We should also get some vegetables. I need carrots and potatoes for dinner tonight. I'm planning to make a vegetable soup.

Lisa: That sounds delicious! I need tomatoes and onions. I want to make pasta with tomato sauce tomorrow. Do you know where the tomatoes are?

Tom: Yes, they're in aisle 3, next to the lettuce and cucumbers. The tomatoes look very fresh today.

Lisa: Great! Now we need to go to the dairy section. I need to buy milk, cheese, and yogurt. What kind of milk do you usually buy?

Tom: I always buy low-fat milk. It's healthier than whole milk. I drink about 2 liters of milk every week.

Lisa: I prefer whole milk because it tastes better. I also need to get some eggs. They're usually near the dairy products.

Tom: Yes, the eggs are right there. Look, there are different sizes - small, medium, and large. Which size do you want?

Lisa: I usually buy large eggs. They're better for baking cakes and cookies. A dozen large eggs costs about 3 dollars.

Tom: Now let's go to the meat section. I need to buy some chicken for dinner. Do you eat meat, Lisa?

Lisa: Yes, I do, but not very often. I prefer fish to meat. I think fish is healthier and easier to cook.

Tom: I agree! Let's get some salmon. It's on sale today - only 8 dollars per kilogram instead of the usual 12 dollars.

Lisa: That's a great deal! We should also check the bread section. I need to buy some bread for breakfast.

Tom: What kind of bread do you like? They have white bread, brown bread, and whole wheat bread.

Lisa: I prefer brown bread because it's more nutritious. My doctor says it's better for my health.

Tom: Good choice! I like whole wheat bread. It has more fiber. Oh, we should also get some butter and jam.

Lisa: Yes, and don't forget we need some rice and pasta. They're in aisle 7, near the canned foods.

Tom: Right! I also want to buy some coffee. I drink coffee every morning. Do you drink coffee or tea?

Lisa: I prefer tea, especially green tea. It helps me relax in the evening. But I also drink coffee sometimes.

Tom: Perfect! I think we have everything on our list now. Let's go to the checkout counter.

Lisa: Wait! I almost forgot - we need some ice cream for dessert. What's your favorite flavor?

Tom: I love chocolate ice cream! But vanilla is also good. What about you?

Lisa: I like strawberry ice cream the best. Let's get all three flavors!

Tom: Great idea! Now we're really ready to pay.

[End of recording]

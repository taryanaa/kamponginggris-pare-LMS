IELTS A2 Listening Test - The World of Animals (Part 3)

[Audio Script - Duration: approximately 2-3 minutes]

Narrator: You will hear a conversation between John and Mary at a wildlife sanctuary. They are talking about endangered animals. Listen carefully and answer questions 11-15.

John: Mary, have you ever visited a wildlife sanctuary?

Mary: Yes, John. I went to one last summer. It was amazing to see so many animals in their natural habitat.

John: What kind of animals did you see?

Mary: I saw some endangered animals like the Bengal tiger and the Asian elephant. They are protected in the sanctuary.

John: Why are these animals endangered?

Mary: Mainly because of habitat loss and poaching. People cut down forests and hunt animals illegally.

John: That's sad. What is being done to protect them?

Mary: The sanctuary has strict rules. They also have rangers who watch over the animals and prevent poaching.

John: Are there any animals that have recovered because of these efforts?

Mary: Yes! The population of the Indian rhinoceros has increased in recent years thanks to conservation programs.

John: That's great news! What about birds? Are there any endangered birds?

Mary: Yes, the Great Indian Bustard is very rare. It's one of the heaviest flying birds but its numbers are very low.

John: How can people help protect these animals?

Mary: People can support wildlife organizations, avoid buying products made from endangered animals, and spread awareness.

John: I hope more people learn about this. Animals are important for our planet.

Mary: Absolutely! We must all do our part to protect wildlife.

IELTS A2 Listening Test - Daily Routines and Activities

[Audio Script - Duration: approximately 3 minutes]

Narrator: You will hear a conversation between Emma and her friend Jake about their daily routines. Listen carefully and answer questions 6-10.

Emma: Hi Jake! I'm curious about your daily routine. What time do you usually wake up?

Jake: Hi Emma! I usually wake up at 7 o'clock in the morning. I like to start my day early. What about you?

Emma: I wake up a bit later, around 7:30. After I wake up, I always brush my teeth and take a shower. Then I have breakfast. What do you eat for breakfast?

Jake: I usually have cereal with milk and a cup of coffee. Sometimes I eat toast with butter and jam. My breakfast is quite simple. Do you cook breakfast?

Emma: Yes, I love cooking! I often make scrambled eggs with toast, or sometimes pancakes on weekends. After breakfast, I go to work by bus. How do you get to work?

Jake: I ride my bicycle to work. It takes about 20 minutes, and it's good exercise. I work in an office from 9 AM to 5 PM. What time do you finish work?

Emma: I finish work at 6 PM. After work, I usually go to the gym three times a week - on Monday, Wednesday, and Friday. On other days, I go home and relax.

Jake: That's great! I don't go to the gym, but I play football with my friends every Tuesday and Thursday evening. We play for about two hours.

Emma: That sounds fun! What do you do for dinner?

Jake: I'm not a good cook, so I often order food online or eat at restaurants. My favorite food is pizza. I probably eat pizza twice a week.

Emma: Oh, I prefer cooking at home. I usually cook dinner around 7:30 PM. I like making pasta, rice dishes, or grilled chicken with vegetables.

Jake: What time do you go to bed?

Emma: I try to go to bed early, around 10:30 PM, because I need at least 8 hours of sleep. I usually read a book or watch TV before sleeping.

Jake: I go to bed later, around 11:30 PM. I like to play video games or listen to music before bed. On weekends, I stay up even later, sometimes until 1 AM.

Emma: Weekends are different for me too. I wake up later, around 9 AM, and I spend time with my family. We often go shopping or visit the park.

Jake: I love weekends! I usually sleep until 10 AM, then I meet my friends. We go to the cinema or play sports together.

Emma: It's interesting how different our routines are, but we both enjoy our free time!

Jake: Exactly! Everyone has their own way of organizing their day.

[End of recording]

IELTS A2 Listening Test - The World of Animals (Part 4)

[Audio Script - Duration: approximately 2-3 minutes]

Narrator: You will hear a conversation between Anna and her grandfather about pets and domestic animals. Listen carefully and answer questions 16-20.

Anna: Grandpa, I want to get a pet. Can you help me choose the right one?

Grandfather: Of course, Anna! Pets are wonderful companions. What kind of pet are you thinking about?

Anna: I'm not sure. Maybe a dog or a cat? What's the difference between them?

Grandfather: Well, dogs are very loyal and active. They need daily walks and lots of attention. Dogs can live for about 12 to 15 years.

Anna: What about cats?

Grandfather: Cats are more independent than dogs. They sleep about 16 hours a day and are very clean animals. They can live up to 18 years or even longer.

Anna: Which one is easier to take care of?

Grandfather: Cats are generally easier because they use a litter box and don't need daily walks. But dogs are better if you want a playful companion.

Anna: What about smaller pets? My friend has a rabbit.

Grandfather: Rabbits are lovely pets! They are quiet and gentle. Rabbits can live for 8 to 12 years. They eat vegetables and hay, and they need a clean cage.

Anna: Do rabbits need much space?

Grandfather: Yes, rabbits need space to hop around. They should have at least 4 hours outside their cage every day for exercise.

Anna: What about birds? I saw some colorful parrots at the pet store.

Grandfather: Parrots are very intelligent and can learn to talk! Some parrots can live for 50 years or more. But they need a lot of social interaction and can be quite noisy.

Anna: Fifty years? That's longer than I've been alive!

Grandfather: Yes, that's why getting a parrot is a big responsibility. They become part of the family for a very long time.

Anna: What about fish? Are they good pets for beginners?

Grandfather: Fish are perfect for beginners! They are peaceful to watch and don't make noise. A goldfish can live for 10 to 30 years if you take good care of it.

Anna: I didn't know fish could live that long! What do they need?

Grandfather: Fish need clean water, the right temperature, and regular feeding. You should clean their tank once a week.

Anna: This is helpful, Grandpa. I think I need more time to decide which pet is right for me.

Grandfather: That's wise, Anna. A pet is a big responsibility, but they bring so much joy and love to our lives.

[End of recording]

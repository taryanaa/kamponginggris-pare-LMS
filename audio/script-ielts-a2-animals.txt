IELTS A2 Listening Test - The World of Animals

[Audio Script - Duration: approximately 3 minutes]

Narrator: You will hear a conversation between Sarah, a student, and Dr. Wilson, a zoologist, about different animals and their habitats. Listen carefully and answer questions 1-5.

Dr. Wilson: Good morning, Sarah. I understand you're interested in learning about animals for your project?

Sarah: Yes, Dr. Wilson. I need to write about different animals and where they live. Can you help me?

Dr. Wilson: Of course! Let's start with some common animals. What would you like to know first?

Sarah: Well, I'm curious about elephants. Where do they live?

Dr. Wilson: Elephants are fascinating creatures. They live in Africa and Asia. African elephants prefer the savanna - that's the grassland with scattered trees. They need lots of space because they're very large animals. An adult elephant can weigh up to 6,000 kilograms!

Sarah: Wow, that's heavy! What about lions?

Dr. Wilson: Lions also live in Africa, mainly in the savanna. They live in groups called prides. A pride usually has about 10 to 15 lions. The female lions, called lionesses, do most of the hunting.

Sarah: That's interesting. I also want to know about penguins. They live in cold places, right?

Dr. Wilson: Exactly! Most penguins live in Antarctica, which is very cold. The temperature there can be minus 40 degrees Celsius. Penguins have thick feathers to keep warm, and they eat fish from the ocean.

Sarah: What about animals that live in the rainforest?

Dr. Wilson: Oh, there are many! Monkeys, for example. They live in the trees of tropical rainforests in South America, Africa, and Asia. They use their long tails to swing from branch to branch. And then there are colorful birds like parrots that also live in rainforest trees.

Sarah: Do any animals live in the desert?

Dr. Wilson: Yes, camels are perfect for desert life. They can survive without water for many days. They store fat in their humps, not water as many people think. Camels live in the deserts of Africa and Asia.

Sarah: What about polar bears?

Dr. Wilson: Polar bears live in the Arctic, in the far north. It's very cold there, with lots of ice and snow. Polar bears are excellent swimmers and they mainly eat seals. Their white fur helps them hide in the snow when they hunt.

Sarah: This is so helpful! One last question - what about dolphins?

Dr. Wilson: Dolphins are marine mammals, which means they live in the ocean but breathe air like us. They're very intelligent and live in groups. You can find dolphins in oceans all around the world, but they prefer warmer waters.

Sarah: Thank you so much, Dr. Wilson. This information will be perfect for my project!

Dr. Wilson: You're welcome, Sarah. Good luck with your project!

[End of recording]
